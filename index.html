The science of a healthy smile

What is
orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a branch of dentistry that focuses on correcting the position of teeth and jaws. Treatment can improve alignment, bite function and the way your smile comes together.

Better alignmentHelps bring crowded or spaced teeth into a healthier position.
Bite functionCan address certain overbites, underbites and crossbites.
Oral hygieneWell-aligned teeth may be easier to clean effectively.
Smile confidenceSupports a smile you feel comfortable sharing.
